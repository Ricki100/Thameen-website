About Thameen Ware

Precious, every day

Thameen (ثمين) means “precious”. We started Thameen Ware to make beautiful fragrance and everyday luxuries easy to find in Harare, at prices that make sense.

Our shelves bring together Arabian perfume houses and designer-inspired scents: rich ouds, soft musks, warm ambers and sweet gourmands, for him, for her, and to share.

Home . Living . Beauty . Essentials

Alongside perfume, we stock everyday home, hygiene and beauty essentials, so you can pick up your signature scent and the things you need in one visit.

Come and see us at Shop 13, Longcheng Plaza, corner Mutley Bend and Samora Machel Avenue in Belvedere, or order on WhatsApp.
